Is it truly 100% free to list my hotel or resort?
Yes, absolutely. Our Free Forever tier is genuinely free. We never charge commission on bookings, transaction percentage fees, or hidden onboarding costs.

When a traveler views your property page or hotel card, they can click to call your front desk or send a direct WhatsApp message. You negotiate and confirm room bookings directly with the guest on your own terms.
