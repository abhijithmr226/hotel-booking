Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful local insights for travelers staying around Jatayu.

What is the best time to visit Jatayu Earth Center?
Early morning or late afternoon (3:30 PM to 6:00 PM) offers the best weather for the cable car ride and panoramic sunset views.
Can I stay near Jatayu Rock and travel to Varkala Beach?
Yes, Varkala Cliff and Beach are only 28 km (45 minutes drive) from Chadayamangalam.
