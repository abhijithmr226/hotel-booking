Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staying near Jatayu

What is the best way to visit Jatayu Earth’s Center from Kollam or Trivandrum?

Jatayu Earth’s Center is located on MC Road (Chadayamangalam), roughly 38 km from Kollam city and 48 km from Trivandrum.

Is Jatayu Earth’s Center open on all days?

Yes, the center and ropeway operate daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
