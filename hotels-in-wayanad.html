Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful local insights for travelers staying around Wayanad.

Which are the best areas to stay in Wayanad?
Vythiri and Lakkidi are ideal for misty rainforests and luxury treehouses; Sultan Bathery and Kalpetta offer central connectivity; Padinjarathara overlooks the Banasura Sagar dam.
Are there resorts with private pool villas in Wayanad?
Yes, Wayanad features top luxury resorts like Mountain Shadows, Morickap, and Vythiri Resort with private plunge pools and jacuzzi suites.
